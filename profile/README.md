# Custom Software Development & AI Product Engineering

We help founders and growing teams turn complex ideas and messy systems into useful digital products. We bring together product thinking, design, and engineering, from the first technical decisions to launch and further development.

[Explore YUAL](https://yual.io/) · [Start a conversation](https://yual.io/contact)

## What we build

- **Web, mobile & MVPs** — Customer-facing applications, SaaS platforms, internal business tools, and focused first versions that help teams test a product before expanding it.
- **AI & connected systems** — AI/ML tools, LLM integrations, practical automation, APIs, and workflows across existing services.
- **Architecture & modernization** — Better foundations for existing products: legacy modernization, architecture improvements, and maintainable code.

## How we work

We start with the problem, business logic, and constraints, then shape the scope and build what matters. Our team covers product planning, UX/UI, frontend, backend, integrations, infrastructure, QA, and post-launch improvements.

Expect direct communication, clear technical decisions, and ownership of the work.
